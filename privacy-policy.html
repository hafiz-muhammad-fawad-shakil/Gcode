GCodex Privacy Policy — TL;DR Version

This application (website and/or program) collects some anonymous data from its users (you). GCodex will not store, upload, or transmit your G-code files — all file processing happens locally in your browser.

Data collected is used exclusively for statistical analytics and tracking application usage and performance.

Personal data is collected for the following purposes by the following services:

Analytics

Google Analytics 4

Personal Data: Cookies and Usage Data. Used to track aggregate site usage, session duration, and browser information. No personally identifiable information is collected.

Advertising

Google AdSense

Personal Data: Cookies and browsing data. Used to serve relevant advertisements to cover hosting and development costs. GCodex does not receive personal data from AdSense beyond aggregate revenue reporting.

Displaying External Content

Additional Fonts

Personal Data: Usage Data and various types of data as specified in the privacy policy of the service (Google Fonts).

File Privacy

100% Local Processing

GCodex processes all files locally inside your browser using JavaScript and WebGL. No file content is ever sent to any server. There is no server-side component that could receive your files.
